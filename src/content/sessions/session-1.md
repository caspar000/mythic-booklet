- Session started in the morning with a mock training battle between the Vulture Knight and the Chain Knight. The Chain Knight easily overpowered the Vulture Knight.
- The Knights decided to move towards the closest Town. During the night after they set up camp a mist engulfed their surroundings and they saw a flock of red crows fly overhead, escaping from something.
- The Knights couldn't sleep and decided to try to get to the Town at night. They were traveling blind and didn't manage to get to the city, losing both Clarity and Spirit.
- In the morning the mist grew thicker, they couldn't see past the horizon. In their desperate attempt to get to the city they traveled in the wrong direction again, keeping the town away from their grasp.
- In the afternoon, the mist grew even thicker and the Knights found themselves surrounded by 6 shadowy stalker entities, who frequently changed their forms. The stalkers tried to get the Dust Knight with their claws when he was separated from the group but he narrowly escaped. The Knights realized that the stalkers only attack them if they are separated so they traveled close together.
- The Gull Knight, with the guidance of his Seer (who Sees through the eyes of all flying beings, in this case, a crow), navigated the party through the thick mist towards the Town.
- The Town was surrounded by a wall with abandoned and overgrown battlements. In the middle of the Town they saw a Gothic Church overflowing with a thousand candles, acting as a seat of power of the town.
- The Knights had a well deserved rest in the Tavern. Something something, 5 old men.
- The next day, the Knights met with The Questing Knight, who inherited the right to rule over the Town from The Candle Knight, who tragically died by the hands of the Hydra 2 ages ago. The Questing Knight directed the Knights towards the Fungal Seer who lived close by and pointed them towards the general direction of two Myths in his area.
- The Knights traveled to the Seer. In his lair, the Knights were overwhelmed by the luminous presence of a turtle like being overgrowing with thousands of mushrooms. They asked the Seer for guidance about the Mist.
- The Knights inhaled the Seer's fungi and were transported into a shared vision where they re-experienced the events of the Mist. They fought and defeated the naked Candle Knight who gave them a cryptic hint about the Mist's origin, but he was slain in a single blow before he could give them proper direction. The Vulture Knight, unsatisfied with the Candle Knight's cryptic guidance, gouged out his intestines and directed them to answer him properly. The intestines declared that The Mist is not natural, and that it is pushed back by smoke from burning bones, south, in the mountainous region on the other side of the river. For a single millisecond, the Knights felt a shared heavenly vision, which was so strong that they awoke from their shared dream.
- The Knights awoke outside the Seer's lair, which was now sealed. They decided to move back to the Town before deciding on a direction, but before they would travel they had to rest for the night, so they decided to make camp in a field of golden flowers.
- The sun didn't set. It stayed on the horizon for the entire night. The Knights were too tired to care.
- In the morning they set out towards the Town.
